ZONEDIM PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 25 October 2026
ZonedimPrivacy policy

Zonedim ("the app") is made by Crocbyte, an independent developer. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device. In short: nothing leaves your device.

What the app collects

Nothing. Zonedim has no internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Accessibility Service

Zonedim uses Android's Accessibility Service only to dim parts of the screen. To decide where to dim and what to keep bright, it looks at:

  • which app is open, and where its windows, the keyboard, the box you type in, pop-ups and video players are on screen;
  • in chat apps you choose, the screen layout, to find where the message list is.

It never reads the text or content of other apps, never stores what is on your screen, and never shares it. All of this happens on your device, in the moment. The app shows this explanation and asks for your consent before you turn the service on, and you can turn it off at any time in Android's Accessibility settings.

Sensors

  • Light sensor: used only while "Follow room light" is on, to adjust the dim strength. Readings are not stored.
  • Motion sensor: used only while "Twist to peek" is on and dimming is active, to notice a wrist twist that briefly brightens the screen. Readings are not stored.

System settings

If you use the optional screen-brightness control, the app asks for "Modify system settings" and changes only your screen brightness (and turns automatic brightness off when you move the brightness bar).

Data stored on your device

Your settings and a local crash log are stored only on your device. They leave it only if you choose "Export settings" or "Share log" yourself. The crash log contains the app's own events and never includes the names of your apps or what is on your screen. The widgets remember the last app you used, in memory only, so "Edit zones" can reopen it; this is forgotten when the device restarts. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

Children

Zonedim is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
