Partial screen dimmer for Android · by Crocbyte
ZonedimPrivacy policy

Zonedim dims parts of your screen instead of all of it. Keep a picture-in-picture video bright while the game behind it dims, dim a chat's messages while the keyboard stays bright, or dim the whole phone at night except the things you choose.

Private by design. No internet permission, no account, no ads, no analytics. Everything stays on your device. Read the privacy policy.
